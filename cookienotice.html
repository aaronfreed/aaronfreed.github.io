Notice on cookie usage

This website uses cookies for one reason and one reason only: to allow users to customize its appearance without having to reselect their choices every time they load a page. They’re also entirely opt-in: they’re only set if users choose non-default settings. They’re stored entirely on users’ local devices, and I’ve implemented no code to track user data – nor could I, since, converted to a human-readable format, they contain only data on:

  • Color scheme: light mode, dark mode, or system default
  • Primary font: FiraGO or Atkinson Hyperlegible Next

(Current defaults are bolded.)

In fact, this site’s server never sees your cookie data: they’re only used to activate embedded client-side browser scripts that alter the appearance without sending a single byte to the server. In less technical terms, your preferences are used exclusively by your browser and are never sent to this website.

To personalize your viewing experience on this site without cookies, you could create a user agent stylesheet, but that’s a complicated process that I wouldn’t recommend to non-programmers, and explaining it is beyond the scope of this notice. Cookies are the best method existing web standards provide for user preferences to persist across multiple pages on a website (or even after a refresh). Sadly, they’re also widely misused for surveillance, leading many users to distrust them across the board. This is one of their many legitimate uses, though.

To summarize, this website’s cookies relate entirely to its appearance, are stored entirely on your device, and are never sent to the website. I don’t store any data on people who view my website and have no interest in tracking their viewing habits, much less profiting off them.
