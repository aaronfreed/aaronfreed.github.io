Τέττᾰρες πλευραί, ῠ̔́μνοι, καιροί, καί ᾰ̓́νεμοι

Téttăres pleuraí, hŭ́mnoi, kairoí, kaí ắnemoi

(Four Sides, Songs, Seasons, & Winds)

an anthology by Aaron Freed

I write a lot of music – as in, just from June 2024 to April 2025, I wrote or cowrote over five hours of original compositions. This collection contains four of my favorite tracks from that time. I’ve always loved old double albums where every LP side is a single track, so as soon as I had that many side-length connections, I felt legally obligated to compile them.

These tracks are, of course, atypical of my work in a few ways:

  • They’re about ten minutes longer than my median track. (The fact I think they’re all worth anthologizing is no coincidence: I only make songs of that length if I think they’re fantastic.)
  • “Omnēs viae Sōlem dūcunt” and «Καταιβᾰτή ἕλῐξ» have vocal parts, which I seldom write.
  • «Καταιβᾰτή ἕλῐξ» is partly based on the work of another composer, Chris Christodoulou (Risk of Rain series, Deadbolt), with his permission. I’ve been known to insert brief quotes of other composers’ work as easter eggs, but I rarely rewrite entire songs.

However, they also showcase several hallmarks of my style for much/all of their runtime:

  • Eschewal of traditional verse/chorus structure
  • Unorthodox chord progressions
  • Complex, fill-laden drum parts
  • Compound time signatures
  • Complex arrangements built around dense arpeggiation
  • Lengthy, gradual, dramatic dynamic shifts
  • mostly instrumental, with lyrics only in foreign languages

Overall, my work is too stylistically diverse to fit under a single umbrella, but these three tracks broadly fit under the umbrellas of progressive electronic, prog rock, and/or post-rock. My biggest conscious influences are:

  1. Johann Sebastian Bach
  2. Brian Eno
  3. The Beatles
  4. Pink Floyd
  5. Nobuo Uematsu
  6. Vangelis
  7. King Crimson
  8. Genesis (& its members’ solo work)

My other influences include Steve Albini, Beastie Boys, Black Sabbath, Chris Christodoulou, John Coltrane, Miles Davis, Deathspell Omega, Bob Dylan, Tim & Geoff Follin, Marvin Gaye, Genesis, Philip Glass, Godspeed You! Black Emperor, Joy Division, Kōenji Hyakkei, Magma, Metallica, Yasunori Mitsuda, Janelle Monáe, Joanna Newsom, Nightwish, Nine Inch Nails, Steve Reich, Ren, Rush, Ringo Shīna, Slint, Talk Talk, Talking Heads, Tears for Fears, Tool, The Velvet Underground, Windir, Yes, Neil Young, & Frank Zappa. Some of these influences are undoubtedly more direct than others: e.g., the Beastie Boys’ influence on my music is probably quite oblique, but Paul’s Boutique has lived rent-free in my head for something like twenty years, and while I could never in a million years imitate Deathspell Omega’s compositional style, they’re probably most responsible for my attempts to combine choral music and metal.
